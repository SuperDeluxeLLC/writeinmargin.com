Type what you want

⌘K knows every scene, person, note and file. Type it, press return, and the caret never moves.

Do, ask or make

Verbs do, questions ask, a file’s name makes it. Each kind of writing offers only its own words.

What to type
  • rename Maya to MiaEvery cue and mention. One undo.
  • todo buy film /propsA to-do, on the Props list.
  • title The Kettle ScreamsThe title page, set.
  • find kettleEvery line that says it.
  • move this scene upAs on the board.
  • goal 90 pagesIn pages, words or minutes.
  • board, outline or readAny view, by name.
  • pdf, Word or ePubAny file the kind makes.
  • settings, or any panelBy its name.

One of three ways

⌘K is one of three ways to write. The other two are on the home page.

A note in a line

Type note, then its title. Return opens it there to write the rest, kept as you type.

The rows are the stacks: the piles notes sit in. Return keeps it in none. A slash and part of a name picks one.

Ask about your script

A ? first, then the question. The reader you choose answers, with the scenes it means.

Nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose a reader. Your private notes never go.

The reader is yours to pick. Apple’s on-device model, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Ollama, or nobody. Ask again and it goes on in the room, a kept chat. ⇧return starts a new one.

Also a menu or panel

⌘K is a shortcut. Everything it does is also a menu item or a panel.

  • The selection menu On the page, over the words you chose.
  • The menu bar On the Mac.
  • The panels The icons down the side of the window.
  • Marks in braces On the page, as you type.
