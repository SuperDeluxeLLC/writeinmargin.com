Read the full policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. Covers Margin for Mac and iPad.

Nothing is collected
Margin has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not track you or keep any record of how you use it. No advertising or analytics code is in the app.
Your writing stays on your device
Everything you write is a file on the Mac or iPad you wrote it on. Margin has no server. Nothing you write is ever sent to us.
When Margin goes online
Only where you choose: a reader, a blog you connect, and a picture you put on a page by its web address. Each talks straight to that service, never through us. Dictation goes to Apple’s speech service only where the device cannot hear.
Asking about your script
Nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose a reader. Your private notes never go. The reader is Apple’s on-device model unless you say otherwise, and it sends nothing anywhere. Apple’s model needs macOS 26 or iPadOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. Without it nobody reads until you choose a reader. You can choose Ollama on your own machine instead, or Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another address you give, with your own key, kept in the keychain. Every request is logged in the project, so you can see what went and when.
Publishing
Connect a blog and press Publish, and that piece goes to that service with the key you gave it, kept in the keychain. Nothing is sent until you press Publish.
A picture by its web address
Put a picture on a page by its web address and Margin fetches it from there to draw the page.
Microphone
Dictation uses the microphone only while you speak, and no recording is kept. Voice memos are heard on the device. So is dictation, where the device can. Where it cannot, Apple’s speech service hears it.
Tell us
Tell us sends nothing by itself. It opens your own mail app with a message ready, addressed to hello@writeinmargin.com, and you decide whether to send it.
Files you export
PDF, Fountain, Final Draft, Word, ePub, Markdown, HTML or your whole library go wherever you put them. From there they are in your hands.
Third parties
The only third party is a reader or a blog you choose. There is no data to sell or share, and none is.
Children
Margin collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
Changes
If a feature ever sends anything off the device, this policy changes first, in plain words.
